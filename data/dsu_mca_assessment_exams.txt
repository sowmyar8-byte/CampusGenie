TITLE: DSU MCA Assessment, Exams and Grading
SOURCE: https://mail.dsu.edu.in/images/CA/handbook/MCA_Handbook.pdf

Assessment structure. Every course is assessed through Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) and the Semester End Examination (SEE). CIA and SEE carry 60% and 40% weightage respectively. The Vice-Chancellor can approve a different ratio in exceptional cases.

CIA components. CIA can include tests, mid-term exams, quizzes, term papers, simulation-based problem solving, open-book tests, mini-projects, seminars, viva-voce and marks for attendance. The course instructor announces the components and their weightage at the start of the semester. A student must take part in all CIA components to be eligible for the SEE, otherwise an NE grade is given. CIA marks cannot be improved, but a student may withdraw and re-register for the course.

Passing a course. A student passes a course by scoring at least 40% when CIA and SEE marks are combined.

Grades. O means 90 to 100 marks (10 points, Outstanding). A+ is 80 to 89 (9 points). A is 70 to 79 (8 points). B+ is 60 to 69 (7 points). B is 55 to 59 (6 points). C is 50 to 54 (5 points). P is 40 to 49 (4 points, Pass). F is below 40 (0 points, Fail). Other grades are AP (audit pass), AF (audit fail), IC (incomplete), NE (not eligible) and W (withdrawn).

CGPA and percentage. A student needs a minimum CGPA of 4 to be eligible for the degree. CGPA is converted to a percentage as (CGPA minus 0.75) multiplied by 10. SGPA is the grade point average for one semester and CGPA covers all completed semesters. Audit courses are not counted.

Failing a course. A student with an F grade must repeat the course until a passing grade is obtained. The course can be cleared in the summer term or in the next appropriate semester, with odd-semester courses taken in odd semesters and even-semester courses in even semesters.

Incomplete grade (IC) and make-up exams. If a student misses the SEE because of a natural calamity, medical emergency or death in the family, a make-up exam is possible with the permission of the Faculty Advisor and the Chairperson or Dean. Such courses get a temporary IC grade. Requests for IC should be made at the earliest and not later than the last day of the SEE.

Results. Results are announced by the Controller of Examinations. Grades can be withheld if the student has unpaid dues or a pending disciplinary action.
