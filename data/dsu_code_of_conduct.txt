TITLE: DSU Department of Computer Applications Code of Conduct
SOURCE: https://mail.dsu.edu.in/images/CA/handbook/MCA_Handbook.pdf

Fees. Students must pay tuition, hostel, bus and mess fees on time as stipulated by the university. If fees are not paid, the student may not be allowed to register for courses, stay in the hostel, attend classes or write examinations.

Exam hall. Mobile phones and other electronic devices are strictly prohibited inside the examination hall. They can be confiscated and the student punished as per university rules. Impersonation in any form can lead to expulsion.

Electronic devices. The Chief Warden, Resident Warden, mentor, faculty or other authorised university staff can check laptops and mobile phones, including photos and videos, if misuse of electronic gadgets is suspected.

Smoking, alcohol and drugs. Smoking, or consuming or distributing tobacco or alcohol on campus, can lead to suspension, debarment or expulsion. Possessing, consuming or distributing narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances is a criminal offence and leads to dismissal.

Property and behaviour. Students must not disturb peace, damage or deface university property, or hurt anyone, including through intimidation, ragging or extortion. Eve teasing, sexual harassment, indecent behaviour and public display of affection are prohibited, and offenders can be expelled and face criminal action.

Communication and refunds. Students and parents agree that the university may send information about studies by email, post or messages. Students should also be aware of the university refund policy for fees paid during enrolment.

Anti-ragging. The National Anti Ragging Helpline number shown on the DSU website is 1800-180-5522.
