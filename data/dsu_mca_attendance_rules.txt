TITLE: DSU MCA Attendance Rules
SOURCE: https://mail.dsu.edu.in/images/CA/handbook/MCA_Handbook.pdf

Minimum attendance. Students must attend all classes they are registered for, including lectures, tutorials, practicals and workshop sessions. A student whose attendance in a course falls below 85% is not allowed to appear for the semester end examination (SEE) in that course and is given an NE (Not Eligible) grade for it.

Condonation of shortage. Up to 10% of attendance shortage can be condoned by the Vice-Chancellor, but only on the specific recommendation of the department Chairman and the Dean, and only for a reasonable cause.

Accepted reasons for condonation. (1) A medical emergency or illness needing rest, certified by a Government doctor only, or a death of a close family member. (2) Representing the university in sports, cultural, extra-curricular or co-curricular activities. (3) Presenting a paper at a national or international conference, or attending a recognised workshop or seminar.

Short leave (less than two weeks). Apply in advance to the Chairman of the department, giving full reasons and supporting documents. The Chairman grants such leave. The attendance and condonation rules still apply.

Long leave (more than two weeks). Apply in advance through the Chairman to the Dean with supporting documents. The Dean decides on the Chairman's recommendation. The attendance and condonation rules still apply.

Informing faculty. It is the student's responsibility to tell the concerned course instructors about the absence before taking leave.

What happens with an NE grade. The student must register for that course again when it is next offered, meet the attendance requirement and pass it. If the NE course is a prerequisite for a course in a later semester, the student cannot register for that connected course until the prerequisite is passed. NE courses can be cleared in the summer term by paying the prescribed fee.

Important note. These rules come from the MCA governing regulations 2021 published in the DSU MCA Handbook. Other DSU programmes can differ, for example the BCA regulations 2025 use a 75% threshold. Always confirm with the latest circular or your department.
