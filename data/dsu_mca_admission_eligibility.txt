TITLE: DSU MCA Admission Eligibility
SOURCE: https://mail.dsu.edu.in/images/CA/handbook/MCA_Handbook.pdf and https://dsu.edu.in/images/Admission/brochures/2026/MCA_Brochure_2026.pdf

First year (first semester) eligibility, MCA regulations 2021. A candidate who has passed a recognised undergraduate degree with Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science, Computer Applications, Computer Programming, Business Mathematics or Business Statistics as one of the optional subjects, with at least 50% aggregate marks, is eligible. The minimum is 45% for SC, ST and Category-I candidates of Karnataka. A candidate who studied and passed one of those subjects in pre-university with 50% (45% for the same categories) can also be considered.

Lateral entry (second year, third semester). A candidate with a recognised bachelor's degree of at least 3 years in BCA or B.Sc (IT or Computer Science), with Mathematics at 10+2 or degree level and at least 50% aggregate marks (45% for SC, ST and Category-I of Karnataka), is eligible.

MCA Brochure 2026. The brochure states that any UG degree such as B.E, B.Tech, B.Sc, B.Com, B.A, B.Voc or BCA is accepted, preferably with Mathematics at 10+2 or graduation level, with 50% marks (45% for SC/ST).

Note. The handbook regulations are from 2021 and the brochure is for the 2026 admissions. For the latest rules, check the official DSU admissions page or the admissions helpline.
