TITLE: DSU MCA Registration, Semesters and Progression
SOURCE: https://mail.dsu.edu.in/images/CA/handbook/MCA_Handbook.pdf

Academic session. Each academic year has an odd semester (September to January), an even semester (February to May) and a summer make-up term (June to August). Each semester is about sixteen weeks long. Exact dates of registration, internal assessment, exams and vacations are given in the Academic Calendar for the session.

Course registration. Students register for approved courses through their Faculty Advisor at the end of the previous semester or in the first week of the current semester, as notified in the academic calendar. Registration is allowed only if the student has cleared all university, hostel and library dues of earlier semesters, paid the required advance dues for the current semester and has not been debarred.

Dropping a course. A course can be dropped within 4 weeks after the semester starts, with permission of the Faculty Advisor and the Chairperson or Dean. A dropped course is not shown on the grade card.

Withdrawing from a course. A course can be withdrawn up to 4 weeks before the start of the semester end examinations, with the same permissions. A W grade is given, and the student must register for the course again when it is offered and pass it.

Credits. One credit equals one lecture hour or one tutorial hour per week, or two hours of laboratory or practical work per week.

Summer term. A student can register for four credit courses or 16 credits, whichever is higher, in a summer term by paying the prescribed fee per credit. Students with NE or F grades can use the summer term to clear those courses.

Termination (Not Fit For The Programme). A student who fails to pass a course even after 4 attempts, or who scores a CGPA below 4.0 four times during the programme, can be terminated as Not Fit For The Programme. The student may appeal to the Vice-Chancellor through the Dean, who may allow 2 additional attempts after a committee review.

Temporary withdrawal. A student can temporarily withdraw for a semester or more because of prolonged illness or a grave family calamity. The application must be made within 6 weeks of the semester start or the last day attended, with documents and the guardian's endorsement. Normally only one such withdrawal is allowed, and there must be no dues.

Degree eligibility. To get the MCA degree a student must complete all credit requirements with a CGPA of 4.0 or higher, finish all mandatory audit courses, and have no dues or pending disciplinary action.
