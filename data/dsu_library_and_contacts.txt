TITLE: DSU Library, Campuses and Contacts
SOURCE: https://dsu.edu.in/student-life/library

Library. The DSU library can seat 560 students at a time. It holds a large collection of books, CDs, DVDs and the latest periodicals, with access to online resources through the Digital Library. It is open to undergraduate and postgraduate students, research scholars and faculty. E-books can be accessed from desktops inside the campus through the library online catalogue. There are constituent libraries for the School of Engineering, School of Computer Application, School of Commerce and Management Studies, College of Journalism and Mass Communication, School of Law, School of Basic and Applied Sciences, School of Design and Digital Trans-media, College of Pharmaceutical Sciences, College of Physiotherapy and College of Nursing Sciences.

Main campus. DSU Main Campus is at Devarakaggalahalli, Harohalli, Kanakapura Road, Bengaluru South District, 562112.

City Innovation Campus. The administrative and main admission office is at Kudlu Gate, Hosur Road, Bengaluru, 560114. The MCA programme under the Department of Computer Applications, School of Engineering lists its address as Kudlu Gate, Hosur Road, Bengaluru 560068.

Admissions contacts. The admissions helpline numbers are 080 46461800 and 080 49092800. Email addresses listed are admissions@dsu.edu.in and enquiry@dsu.edu.in.
