CAMPUS FACILITIES AND SERVICES

1. Canteen
The canteen is open from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM on working days.

2. Medical room
A medical room with a nurse is open from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on working days. A visiting doctor is available on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM. In an emergency, students should inform the nearest faculty member or the security desk immediately.

3. Computer labs
Computer labs are open from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on working days. Students who need to use a lab outside these hours must obtain written permission from a faculty member or the lab in-charge.

4. Wi-Fi
Campus Wi-Fi can be accessed with the student portal username and password. Each student may connect up to 2 devices at a time. Sharing login credentials is not allowed.

5. Transport
University buses run on fixed routes. Students must register at the transport office at the start of the academic year to use the bus service, and must carry their ID card while travelling.

6. Placement cell
The placement cell organises training sessions, company visits and recruitment drives. Students must register with the placement cell in their final year and attend the pre-placement training sessions.

7. ID card replacement
A lost ID card can be replaced by applying at the administrative office with a written declaration and a fee of Rs. 200. A temporary pass is issued until the new card is ready.

8. Grievances
Students can submit grievances through the online grievance form on the student portal or in the grievance box near the administrative office. The grievance cell aims to respond within 7 working days.
