ATTENDANCE POLICY

1. Minimum attendance
Every student must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each course, counted separately for theory classes and laboratory sessions, to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examination.

2. Condonation of shortage
Students whose attendance is between 65% and 74% may apply for condonation of shortage. The application must be submitted to the Head of Department within 5 working days after the attendance shortage list is published, along with a valid reason and supporting documents. Condonation is not automatic and is decided by the Head of Department. Students with attendance below 65% are not eligible for condonation and must repeat the course in a later semester.

3. Medical leave
A student absent due to illness must submit a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner to the class coordinator within 7 days of returning to classes. Medical leave is recorded as an approved absence but the days still count towards the attendance percentage unless the Head of Department approves condonation.

4. On-duty attendance
Students who represent the university in sports, cultural events, hackathons or approved competitions are granted on-duty attendance, provided they obtain written permission from the Head of Department before the event and submit a participation certificate within 5 working days after returning.

5. Checking attendance
Attendance is updated by faculty on the student portal. Students can view course-wise attendance after logging in to the portal. If a student finds an error, it must be reported to the course faculty within 3 working days.

6. Attendance shortage list
The list of students with attendance below 75% is published on the student portal and the department notice board 10 days before the end-semester examinations begin.
