ACADEMIC CALENDAR (ODD SEMESTER, APPROXIMATE)

Semester classes begin in the first week of August.
Fee payment window: first 2 weeks after classes begin.
Project guide allotment for mini projects: end of the second week of the semester.
First internal test: second week of September.
Second internal test: last week of October.
Assignment submission window for the semester: up to the last week of November.
Last working day of the semester: mid November.
Attendance shortage list published: 10 days before the end-semester examinations.
Hall tickets available on the student portal: at least 3 days before the first examination.
End-semester examinations: begin in the first week of December.
Results: normally published within 4 weeks of the last examination.
Even semester classes begin in the first week of February.
Official dates may change. Students must always check the latest circular on the student portal.
