EXAMINATION RULES AND REGULATIONS

1. Evaluation scheme
Each course is evaluated out of 100 marks: 50 marks for continuous internal assessment (two internal tests and assignments) and 50 marks for the end-semester examination. To pass a course, a student must score at least 40% in the end-semester examination and at least 40% in the total.

2. Hall ticket
The hall ticket can be downloaded from the student portal at least 3 days before the first examination. Students must carry a printed copy along with their university ID card. A student without a hall ticket will not be allowed into the examination hall, but may collect a duplicate from the examination section on the same day.

3. Reporting time
Students must reach the examination hall at least 20 minutes before the start of the exam. Entry is not permitted after 30 minutes from the start of the examination. No student may leave the hall during the first 30 minutes.

4. Items not allowed
Mobile phones, smart watches, earphones, notes, books and unauthorised calculators are not permitted inside the examination hall. Such items found with a student will be treated as malpractice, even if unused.

5. Malpractice
Any student found copying, helping others, impersonating or carrying unauthorised material will be reported to the Examination Malpractice Committee. Penalties may range from cancellation of that paper to cancellation of the entire semester examinations, depending on the severity.

6. Absence due to illness
A student who misses an examination because of serious illness may apply for a make-up examination within 5 days of the missed exam, with a medical certificate and the recommendation of the Head of Department.

7. Results and revaluation
Results are published on the student portal, normally within 4 weeks after the last examination. A student may apply for revaluation within 7 days of result publication by paying a fee of Rs. 500 per course through the fee portal.

8. Supplementary examinations
Students who fail a course may appear for the supplementary examination conducted after the declaration of results. The same minimum pass criteria apply.
