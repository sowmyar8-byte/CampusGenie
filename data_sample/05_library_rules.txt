LIBRARY RULES

1. Timings
The library is open from 8:30 AM to 8:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays. During end-semester examination weeks, the library remains open until 10:00 PM.

2. Borrowing
A student may borrow up to 4 books at a time for 14 days using the university ID card. Reference books, journals and previous question papers can be read inside the library only.

3. Renewal
A borrowed book may be renewed once for 7 more days, provided no other student has reserved it. Renewal can be requested at the library desk or through the student portal.

4. Fines
An overdue book is charged a fine of Rs. 2 per day per book. A student with unpaid library fines will not receive a no-dues certificate.

5. Lost or damaged books
A student who loses or damages a book must replace it with the same edition or pay its current cost plus a processing fee of Rs. 100.

6. Digital library
Students can access e-books and online journals through the digital library by logging in with their student portal credentials. Login details must not be shared with others.

7. Conduct
Silence must be maintained in the reading hall. Food, drinks and mobile phone calls are not allowed inside the library. Discussion rooms for group study can be booked at the library desk for up to 2 hours.
