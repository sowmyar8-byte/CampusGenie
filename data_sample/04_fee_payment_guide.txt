FEE PAYMENT GUIDE

1. How to pay
Tuition and other fees are paid through the university online fee portal using UPI, net banking, debit card or credit card. Cash payments are not accepted at the accounts office.

2. Due date
Semester fees must be paid within the first 2 weeks after the semester begins, or by the date announced in the fee circular.

3. Late fee
A late fee of Rs. 100 per day applies after the due date, up to a maximum of Rs. 2000 per semester.

4. Fee receipt
After a successful payment, the receipt can be downloaded from the fee portal under the Payment History section. Students should keep a copy of every receipt.

5. Payment failure
If money is deducted from the bank account but the portal shows the payment as unsuccessful, wait for 48 hours for the amount to be updated. If it is still not updated, contact the accounts office with the transaction ID and a screenshot of the bank message.

6. Refund
A refund request must be submitted in writing to the accounts office within 15 days of payment. Refunds are processed within 30 working days after approval.

7. Scholarships
Students who wish to apply for a scholarship must apply through the scholarship cell before the announced deadline and submit an income certificate and previous semester marks card.

8. Accounts office timings
The accounts office is open from 9:30 AM to 4:30 PM, Monday to Friday. It is closed on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays.
